# Ivory Hotel / Restaurant --- Website Mockup Requirements

**Project:** Sales Mockup Website\
**Purpose:** Create a premium visual prototype that can be shown to the
business owner before any production development is agreed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Project Objective

Create a modern, premium hospitality website that makes the business
look trustworthy, established, welcoming, and worth visiting.

This is **not** the final production website yet. It is a high-quality
sales prototype designed to demonstrate what the business could have
instead of the current broken/outdated web presence.

The mockup must:

-   Look excellent on mobile and desktop.
-   Make the business immediately understandable.
-   Make it easy to contact, locate, and book/visit the business.
-   Put photography and atmosphere ahead of excessive text.
-   Feel like a real hospitality brand, not a generic website template.
-   Be fast and visually polished.
-   Be simple enough to reuse as a foundation for future
    restaurant/hotel clients.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Design Direction

## Overall Feel

**Premium African hospitality + modern editorial design.**

The website should feel:

-   Warm
-   Sophisticated
-   Welcoming
-   Elegant
-   Trustworthy
-   Photographic
-   Contemporary
-   Locally grounded

Avoid:

-   Generic corporate SaaS styling
-   Overly colorful UI
-   Excessive gradients
-   Cheap-looking animations
-   Huge amounts of text
-   Generic stock-template appearance
-   Excessive rounded cards
-   Dashboard aesthetics
-   Cluttered navigation

The website should feel like a place people would actually want to
visit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Visual Language

## Color Direction

Primary palette:

-   Deep charcoal / near-black
-   Warm ivory / off-white
-   Soft beige / sand
-   Muted gold or bronze accents only where appropriate

Accent color should be restrained.

The design should primarily use **neutral hospitality colors** rather
than bright startup colors.

### Light sections

Warm ivory / off-white backgrounds.

### Dark sections

Deep charcoal backgrounds with warm white typography.

### Accent

Use a subtle premium accent for:

-   Buttons
-   Small dividers
-   Active states
-   Important details

Do not allow the accent color to dominate the interface.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Typography

Use a premium serif for major headings and an elegant sans-serif for
body/UI text.

Suggested pairing:

**Headings:** Fraunces / Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond\
**Body/UI:** Inter / Manrope / DM Sans

Typography hierarchy should be obvious:

-   Large editorial hero heading
-   Medium section headings
-   Small uppercase labels
-   Comfortable body text
-   Clear buttons

Avoid excessive font weights.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Navigation

Desktop navigation:

**Logo \| Rooms / Stay \| Dining \| Experience \| Gallery \| Contact \|
Book Now**

Depending on what the business actually offers, labels can be adjusted.

The navigation should be:

-   Minimal
-   Transparent/overlayed over the hero initially
-   Become solid when scrolling
-   Sticky
-   Easy to understand

Primary CTA:

**Book Now**

Secondary CTA:

**Contact Us**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Hero Section

The hero is the most important section.

Use a **full-bleed, high-quality hospitality image**.

Possible imagery:

-   Exterior of the property
-   Beautiful dining area
-   Signature dish
-   Hotel room
-   Evening ambience
-   Guests enjoying the space

The image should communicate the experience immediately.

### Hero layout

Large heading:

> **A Place to Stay. A Place to Gather.**

or a business-specific equivalent once the actual brand positioning is
confirmed.

Supporting copy:

> Discover warm hospitality, memorable dining, and a comfortable place
> to make yourself at home.

Primary CTA:

**Book Your Stay**

Secondary CTA:

**Explore**

Hero should include a subtle dark image overlay to guarantee text
readability.

Do not place the content inside a floating white card.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Trust / Quick Information Strip

Immediately below the hero, introduce a clean information strip.

Example:

**Comfortable Rooms**\
Thoughtfully prepared spaces for your stay.

**Dining**\
Freshly prepared meals and memorable flavours.

**Convenient Location**\
Easy to find and close to key destinations.

**Hospitality**\
A welcoming experience from arrival to departure.

Use simple typography/icons rather than large cards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Introduction Section

A two-column editorial section.

Left:

Large image.

Right:

Small eyebrow:

**WELCOME**

Heading:

> **Hospitality Designed Around You**

Body copy should briefly explain the business.

Include:

-   What the property is
-   What makes it different
-   Location/context
-   Hospitality philosophy

CTA:

**Discover More**

The section should have generous whitespace.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Accommodation Section

If the business provides accommodation, showcase rooms.

Heading:

**Stay With Us**

Each room should have:

-   Large image
-   Room name
-   Short description
-   Starting price if confirmed
-   Key amenities
-   CTA

Example:

**Deluxe Room**

-   Comfortable bed
-   Private bathroom
-   Wi-Fi
-   Air conditioning
-   Breakfast availability

CTA:

**View Room**

Use large photography rather than dense cards.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Dining Section

If the business includes a restaurant, make dining a major section.

Heading:

**Taste the Experience**

Show:

-   Signature dishes
-   Dining room
-   Chef/preparation imagery
-   Cuisine identity
-   Restaurant atmosphere

Possible CTA:

**View Menu**

Secondary CTA:

**Reserve a Table**

The restaurant section should make food photography visually dominant.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 11. Signature Experience Section

Create a section that answers:

> Why should someone choose this place?

Possible content:

-   Chinese cuisine / signature cuisine
-   Hospitality
-   Private dining
-   Events
-   Business meetings
-   Family gatherings
-   Accommodation
-   Convenient location

Use 3--4 carefully selected experiences.

Do not use generic marketing claims without evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 12. Gallery

Large editorial image gallery.

Preferred layout:

-   One large feature image
-   Several supporting images
-   Asymmetric layout
-   Full-width photography
-   Minimal borders

Gallery categories can include:

**Rooms \| Dining \| Property \| Experience**

Clicking an image should open a lightbox in the production version.

For the mockup, visual presentation is the priority.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 13. Location Section

Location should be highly visible.

Include:

**Find Us**

-   Physical location
-   Nearby landmark
-   Opening hours where relevant
-   Phone
-   WhatsApp
-   Google Maps CTA

Include a map placeholder or embedded Google Maps area.

CTA:

**Get Directions**

This is especially important for restaurants and hotels because visitors
often discover them through Google Maps.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 14. Booking / Contact CTA

Create a strong dark-background CTA section.

Example:

> **Ready to experience it for yourself?**

Supporting text:

> Make a reservation, plan your stay, or get in touch with our team.

Buttons:

**Book Now**

**WhatsApp Us**

The WhatsApp CTA should be especially prominent for the Kenyan market.

# 16. Mobile Design

Mobile is a first-class experience, not a compressed desktop website.

Requirements:

-   Sticky mobile navigation
-   Logo
-   Hamburger menu
-   Persistent/accessible booking CTA
-   Large readable typography
-   Touch-friendly buttons
-   No horizontal overflow
-   Fast image loading
-   Properly cropped photography
-   Comfortable spacing

Hero should remain visually powerful on a phone.

The mobile menu should contain:

-   Home
-   Stay
-   Dining
-   Experience
-   Gallery
-   Contact
-   Book Now

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 17. Interaction & Animation

Animations should be subtle.

Use:

-   Fade-in on scroll
-   Gentle image reveal
-   Slight image scale on hover
-   Smooth navigation transitions
-   Button hover states
-   Sticky navigation transition

Avoid:

-   Excessive bouncing
-   Long loading animations
-   Distracting parallax
-   Animations on every element
-   Anything that slows the page

The website should feel premium because of **design and photography**,
not animation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 18. Performance Requirements

The production architecture should be lightweight.

Requirements:

-   Responsive images
-   WebP/AVIF where appropriate
-   Lazy-load images below the fold
-   Avoid unnecessary JavaScript
-   Avoid huge video backgrounds unless they materially improve the
    experience
-   Optimize fonts
-   Minimize third-party scripts
-   Fast first paint

The mockup should already demonstrate good performance practices.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 19. SEO Foundation

Even the mockup should be structured with SEO in mind.

Include:

-   Proper page title
-   Meta description
-   One H1
-   Logical H2/H3 hierarchy
-   Descriptive image alt text
-   Local business information
-   Location keywords naturally
-   Restaurant/hotel-specific structured data in production
-   Open Graph metadata

Potential search intent:

-   `[Business Name] Nairobi`
-   `[Business Name] restaurant`
-   `[Business Name] hotel`
-   `Chinese restaurant Nairobi`
-   `hotel in [location]`
-   `restaurants near [location]`

Do not keyword-stuff.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 20. WhatsApp Integration

Because this is a Kenyan business website, WhatsApp should be treated as
a primary conversion channel.

Examples:

**Chat on WhatsApp**

**Book via WhatsApp**

**Ask About Availability**

The WhatsApp button should open a pre-filled message where appropriate.

Example:

> Hello, I would like to make a reservation at \[Business Name\].

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 21. Booking Flow

For the mockup, do not build a complex booking engine unless
specifically required.

The first version can demonstrate:

**Book Now → Contact/WhatsApp/Booking Form**

Potential production flow:

1.  Select service/room/table
2.  Select date
3.  Select number of guests
4.  Enter contact details
5.  Submit request
6.  Receive confirmation

The actual booking mechanism should be determined after speaking with
the owner.

# 23. Mockup Content Rule

For the sales prototype:

**Do not invent important business facts.**

If information is unknown, use clearly marked placeholder content or
omit the section.

Do not invent:

-   Prices
-   Awards
-   Certifications
-   Room amenities
-   Opening hours
-   Number of rooms
-   Claims about history
-   Customer reviews
-   Star ratings

The mockup should look real without making false claims.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 24. Conversion Strategy

Every major section should move the visitor toward one of three actions:

### 1. Book

**Book Now**

### 2. Contact

**WhatsApp / Call**

### 3. Visit

**Get Directions**

Do not overwhelm visitors with ten different CTAs.

# 26. Technical Stack

For the mockup:

-   React / Next.js
-   Tailwind CSS or existing project styling system
-   Responsive CSS
-   Optimized images
-   Lucide icons or similarly minimal icon set

Avoid adding unnecessary dependencies.

The implementation should be component-based so the template can later
be reused for another client.

Suggested components:

``` text
Navbar
Hero
TrustStrip
AboutSection
RoomSection
DiningSection
ExperienceSection
Gallery
LocationSection
BookingCTA
Footer
MobileMenu
```

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 27. Reusability

This project should eventually become a reusable **hospitality website
system**.

Business-specific information should be easy to replace:

``` text
businessName
logo
heroImage
description
location
phone
whatsapp
rooms
menu
gallery
socialLinks
openingHours
```

Do not hard-code the entire design around one business.

The first mockup can be customized heavily, but the underlying
components should remain reusable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 28. Design Quality Bar

The final mockup should make the owner think:

> "This looks like a serious website for my business."

It should **not** look like:

> "Someone made a generic website template."

The strongest visual priorities are:

1.  Photography
2.  Typography
3.  Spacing
4.  Layout
5.  Brand atmosphere
6.  Clear calls to action
7.  Mobile quality
8.  Performance

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 29. What We Are NOT Building Yet

Do not spend time on:

-   Complex admin dashboards
-   User authentication
-   Full custom booking engines
-   Payment processing
-   Customer accounts
-   CMS development
-   Advanced analytics
-   Complex backend architecture
-   Unnecessary animations

Those are production discussions after the business shows interest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 30. Sales Prototype Definition of Done

The mockup is ready to show the owner when:

-   [ ] Desktop homepage looks premium
-   [ ] Mobile homepage looks equally intentional
-   [ ] Hero immediately communicates the business
-   [ ] Business identity is clear
-   [ ] Navigation works
-   [ ] CTA buttons work
-   [ ] WhatsApp CTA is wired or clearly demonstrated
-   [ ] Location section exists
-   [ ] Gallery looks professional
-   [ ] Restaurant/hotel offering is clearly presented
-   [ ] No obvious broken states
-   [ ] No horizontal scrolling
-   [ ] Images are properly cropped
-   [ ] Typography is polished
-   [ ] Loading experience is acceptable
-   [ ] No fake claims have been presented as facts
-   [ ] The entire site feels like one coherent brand

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 31. Core Principle

**We are not trying to impress the owner with code.**

We are trying to make them visualize:

> **"This could be my business online."**

The website should sell the experience of the business before it sells
the technology used to build it.
